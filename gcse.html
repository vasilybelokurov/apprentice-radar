Cambridge: apprenticeships open with GCSEs

Open apprenticeships within 5 miles of Cambridge, at Levels 2 to 5, where the employer's essential entry requirements are no more than GCSEs or Functional Skills. Anyone aged 16 or over can apply for an apprenticeship.

  • Vacancies that need A levels, a T Level, a BTEC, UCAS points or a vocational qualification are left out (listed at the bottom).
  • Degree apprenticeships (Levels 6 and 7) are left out: they normally need A levels or equivalent.
  • Many vacancies ask for particular GCSE grades, usually 4 (C) in English and maths. Check the requirements on each card.
  • Requirements are as the employer wrote them. Check the official page before applying.
